## 🟩 Chroma Key Editing

Chroma key editing is used when a subject is recorded in front of a solid color background, commonly green. Movavi-Effects provides controls for selecting that background color and reducing it so the subject can appear over a different scene. The process is visual: place the green-screen clip on the timeline, open the chroma key controls, and choose the background color.

Use the preview window to check the edges around hair, shoulders, and moving objects. Small adjustments usually work better than large ones. If the subject still shows a green fringe, refine the color selection or adjust the softness controls. The goal is a clean result that looks natural in the preview. When the background is removed, add the replacement image or video on the layer below.

For best results, use evenly lit footage. Shadows on the background can make removal harder. If your first attempt looks uneven, try a different frame in the clip where the background is clearer, then apply the setting again.
